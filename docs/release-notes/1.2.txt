Looping in one step. Drag across the waveform to mark a passage, then tap Loop — that is the whole of it. Drag a new passage while looping and it loops straight away. Loop edges carry grip handles and are easier to catch with a finger, and a tap only moves the playhead, so a loop you set is never cleared by accident. On iPhone and iPad, Clear Loop is in the ⋯ menu and on a long press of the Loop button.

Tap the overview strip at the top to jump the playhead there.
